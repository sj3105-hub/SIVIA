About Sivia Semiconductor

Who We Are

A startup dedicated to solving the critical reliability gap in autonomous systems and modern silicon architectures.

The Reliability Challenge in Modern Computing

As silicon process geometries shrink and mission-critical systems operate under extreme environmental demands—such as high vibrational stress in autonomous aerial vehicles, analog thermal drift, or intense read cycles in AI memory—hardware components inevitably degrade.

Today, the majority of computing architectures operate under rigid binary pass/fail gates: a subsystem is assumed fully healthy until it catastrophically breaks. Software-based diagnostics often intervene too late or lack direct visibility into the sub-clock physical degradation of silicon circuits.

Sivia Semiconductor was founded to introduce continuous, graded health awareness into silicon. By embedding telemetry capture and degradation scoring directly within synthesizable RTL logic, our architectures empower mission controllers and host processors to receive advance risk metrics and execute coordinated, graceful operational holds before catastrophic failure occurs.

Synthesizable & Fabless

Focused entirely on RTL intellectual property (Verilog/SystemVerilog) designed for target-agnostic integration across FPGAs, SoCs, and ASICs.

Hardware-Speed Awareness

Captures error syndromes, telemetry indicators, and drift patterns at native hardware frequencies, bypassing slow OS-level monitoring bottlenecks.

Mission & Vision

The core philosophy guiding Sivia Semiconductor's engineering roadmap.

Our Mission

Engineer Self-Aware Silicon

"To develop synthesizable, lightweight, and reusable hardware IP blocks that transform rigid semiconductor components into self-monitoring, fault-aware subsystems capable of real-time degradation assessment and graceful operational recovery."

Core R&D Focus Areas

As an early-stage startup, our architectural modeling and hardware explorations center on three high-consequence domains where silicon reliability is critical:

01

Fault-Aware Hardware Architectures

Researching synthesizable telemetry blocks that continuously aggregate soft-error patterns, parity syndrome signatures, and subsystem stress indicators to compute real-time, graded degradation scores directly in silicon.

Autonomous UAV & Avionics Reliability
